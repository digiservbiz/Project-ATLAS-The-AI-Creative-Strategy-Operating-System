# 01 — Executive Summary

## 1. Product

ATLAS (AI Creative Strategy Operating System) is a multi-agent platform designed to help e-commerce operators, brands, agencies, and creative teams discover, formulate, test, and improve direct-response creative strategies.

The system combines structured product intelligence, market research, creative strategy, generative ideation, QA, memory, evaluation, and workflow orchestration.

## 2. Problem

Creative teams commonly suffer from fragmented research, repetitive ideation, weak continuity between ads and landing pages, unstructured testing, poor evidence discipline, and loss of learning between campaigns. A tool that only generates scripts or edits videos does not solve the strategy problem.

ATLAS addresses the upstream decision layer: **what should be said, to whom, why it should work, what evidence supports it, how it should be tested, and what should be learned from the result.**

## 3. Primary users

### E-commerce operator
Needs rapid creative hypotheses, testing plans, and actionable campaign assets without hiring a full strategy team.

### Creative strategist
Needs research, angle discovery, structured ideation, QA, and reusable knowledge without losing strategic control.

### Agency/team lead
Needs repeatable workflows, approvals, audit trails, quality standards, and client/project separation.

### Creative producer
Needs strategy-backed briefs, hooks, scripts, shot concepts and production-ready instructions.

## 4. Value proposition

ATLAS should reduce the time between product understanding and high-quality creative testing while improving strategic consistency and learning capture.

It should not promise a guaranteed ROAS, CPA reduction, or winning creative. Its role is to improve the quality and throughput of hypotheses and decision-making.

## 5. Product outcomes

A successful ATLAS run should produce a traceable campaign strategy package containing:

- product/customer understanding;
- research findings and evidence;
- key objections and skepticism risks;
- prioritized creative strategy;
- a diverse angle matrix;
- hook concepts;
- creative/script concepts;
- offer and landing-page continuity recommendations;
- QA findings;
- test hypotheses;
- required evidence/assets;
- approval status;
- provenance for important claims.

## 6. Non-goals for v1

- Fully autonomous ad spending.
- Autonomous publication of consequential campaigns without approval.
- Fabricated market/customer evidence.
- Replacing video production software.
- Claiming that AI-generated creative is inherently superior to human creative.
- Supporting every advertising platform on day one.

## 7. Product philosophy

ATLAS should behave like a disciplined senior strategy team rather than a single prompt. Specialists have explicit responsibilities, tools are permissioned, outputs are versioned, decisions are traceable, and evaluation is part of the product rather than an afterthought.
